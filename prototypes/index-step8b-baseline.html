UX decisions

One account can own personal and business cards. Saved contacts stay private. Public discovery is opt-in. Cards remain reachable by shared links when set to unlisted.

Navigation keeps the three core actions one tap away: My Card, Saved, Discover.

The QR graphic, locations, contact actions, businesses and analytics shown here are illustrative. No Firebase account or backend is connected.
